Now open

Canyon Ranch Austin opened its doors on October 15

The 600-acre wellness resort at 600 Moonlight Bend is officially welcoming guests. It has 141 rooms, a 40,000-square-foot spa the company calls the largest in Texas, and 1.5 miles of Lake Travis shoreline on the former Flying X ranch. Rates start at $1,325 a night. A 134-home residential community surrounds the resort.

Sources: Texas Monthly · CultureMap

Development · Oct 10

New restaurant and retail building planned at 5600 Hwy 71

A state filing shows SpiceQ plans a 10,008-square-foot restaurant and retail building on the Hwy 71 corridor. No tenants announced yet.

Community

The new Spicewood Community Library is under construction

Work began in January on a 6,000-square-foot library at 1011 Spur 191, built behind the current building so it stays open. State records list completion around January 2027. The Friends group is still fundraising.

Drinks

Bella Loma Distillery's tasting room is pouring agave spirits

The family-owned distillery serves house-made blanco and reposado in margaritas and Mexican martinis, with añejo, mezcal and whiskey planned.
