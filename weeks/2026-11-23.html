Coming up · Dec 4–5

One weekend, two holiday traditions: Light Up Parade and the Spicewood Holiday Market

Mark the first weekend of December. On Friday, December 4, the Light Up Parade rolls back through downtown Marble Falls along Main Street, timed to pair with the Walkway of Lights, which opened for the season last week at Lakeside Park. The city hasn't posted a start time yet, so check its parade page closer to the date.

Closer to home, Mic Drop Creative on Hwy 71 hosts the Spicewood Holiday Market that same Friday and again on Saturday, December 5. It's a small, curated gathering of local makers and growers, set up inside and out under the oaks, with handmade gifts, art, vintage pieces and locally grown goods.

Put them together and you have an easy holiday outing: shop local in Spicewood during the day, then head up 71 for the parade and the lights after dark.

Sources: City of Marble Falls (Light Up Parade) · AllEvents (Spicewood Holiday Market)

Business · Hwy 71

Firehouse Coffee Co. opens a drive-thru in Spicewood

A fire department-themed drive-thru coffee shop is now open on Hwy 71 in Spicewood. The first-responder- and veteran-owned business opens at 5:30 AM every day, which makes it an early option for commuters heading toward Austin.

Development · Hwy 71

SpiceQ restaurant and retail project planned at 5600 Hwy 71

A state filing shows a roughly $1.8 million mixed-use restaurant and retail development called SpiceQ is planned for 5600 Hwy 71 in Spicewood, with construction expected to begin in January.

Live music · December

Gary P. Nunn and a Fleetwood Mac tribute headline Poodie's December

Poodie's Hilltop Roadhouse has its December calendar up. Texas music icon Gary P. Nunn plays December 18, and Gypsy ATX brings a Fleetwood Mac tribute for New Year's Eve. The roadhouse is closed on Christmas Day and keeps holiday hours on Christmas Eve.
