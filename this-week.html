Breaking ground

Pickleball Grove breaks ground in Spicewood, with outdoor courts aiming for December

Work started October 12 on Pickleball Grove at 5411 Reimers Peacock Road. The first phase is four outdoor pickleball courts, which the team hopes to open in mid to late December.

An indoor building is planned next, with eight pickleball courts (four built for championship play), three padel courts, locker rooms with showers, and a mezzanine lounge with ping-pong and a private meeting room. Every court is set to have PlaySight video cameras. The indoor side is targeted for late February 2027, and a state filing puts the project at about $6.9 million.

Expect open play, clinics and private lessons once it opens.

Halloween · Oct 31

Two ways to spend Halloween Saturday in Spicewood

Iron Wolf Ranch hosts a free afternoon show with Brandon Alan from 2 to 5 PM. After dark, Poodie's throws a Halloween party and costume contest with Madam Radar at 10:30 PM ($20).

Library · Oct 29

Pumpkin Parade and ghost stories at Lake Travis Library

Kids can join a Pumpkin Parade at 10:30 AM on October 29, with the West branch on Hwy 71 in Spicewood taking part, followed by a Ghost Tales storyteller on October 30. A beginner watercolor class is also on the calendar at Library West on October 19.
