This Friday

Walkway of Lights opens Friday with new displays and a new host

The Walkway of Lights at Lakeside Park in Marble Falls switches on for the season this Friday, November 20, at 6 PM. It's an easy drive up Hwy 71 from Spicewood and one of the Highland Lakes' best-loved holiday outings.

This is the first year the City of Marble Falls is running the show. The Chamber of Commerce, which managed the walkway for more than three decades, handed it over this summer and is advising during the transition. The city set aside roughly $450,000 for new sculptures from Mosca Design, which will add a dozen themed areas with large pieces and spots made for photos.

One change to know before you go: the ice rink is not coming back this year. City leaders said they wanted to put the focus on the lights, and a new LED feature will fill the space where the rink used to be.

Sources: City of Marble Falls · DailyTrib (new displays, no ice rink) · DailyTrib (funding approved)

Winston's Spicewood spot is now Neighbor's Cafe

The cafe at 4900 Bee Creek Road changed hands in October and now operates as Neighbor's Cafe. Winston's owners are concentrating on their Bee Cave location instead, according to Community Impact's latest business roundup.

Spicewood Holiday Market set for early December

Mic Drop Creative on Hwy 71 hosts a small, curated holiday market December 4 and 5, with local makers and growers set up inside and under the oaks out front. Expect handmade jewelry, candles, art, vintage finds and locally grown goods.
