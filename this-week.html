Canyon Ranch Austin opens its doors on Lake Travis in Spicewood

After years of construction, the Canyon Ranch wellness resort on the former Flying X Ranch began welcoming guests in late October. The property spreads across more than 600 acres in Spicewood, with roughly a mile and a half of Lake Travis shoreline.

The resort has 141 guest rooms and a spa of about 40,000 square feet with 40 treatment rooms, which CultureMap reports is the largest in Texas. Dining includes Estella, a signature restaurant whose menu is guided by the chef behind San Francisco's three-Michelin-star Californios, plus a cafe, a lounge and an outdoor grove for special dinners.

Stays are all-inclusive and start at $1,325 per person per night. A collection of 134 single-family homes is also planned on the property.

Sources: CultureMap · Texas Monthly

Development · Hwy 71

SpiceQ mixed-use project proposed at 5600 Hwy 71

A state filing shows a planned $1.8 million development with restaurant and retail space on Hwy 71 in Spicewood. Construction is penciled in to start in January, though the project is still tentative.
