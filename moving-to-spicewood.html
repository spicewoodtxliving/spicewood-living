Moving to Spicewood

The straight answers before you move

Spicewood isn't a city. It's an unincorporated community spread across county lines, which shapes your schools, your taxes, and your utilities. Here's what to check.

UnincorporatedNo city government. County rules apply.
2 school districtsMarble Falls ISD or Lake Travis ISD, by address
Septic & wellsCommon on acreage. Ask before you buy.
MUDs & POAsSome subdivisions add tax districts or dues

Where people live

TRAVIS COUNTY · LAKE TRAVIS ISD · HOA

West Cypress Hills

Master-planned community off Hwy 71 with greenbelts, trails, resort-style and lap pools, sport courts, and an elementary school inside the neighborhood.

LAKE TRAVIS ACCESS

Lakeside Beach

Small subdivision with its own three-quarter-mile stretch of Lake Travis shoreline, a community pool and a boat ramp.

BURNET COUNTY · MARBLE FALLS ISD · POA

Spicewood Trails

Custom-home acreage lots, bring your own builder. Underground utilities, fiber internet, dark-sky lighting rules, a 3-acre park and trails. Most lots need septic.

AREA-WIDE

No-HOA acreage

Land and homes with no association dues or architectural rules. More freedom, more responsibility: check water source, septic, road maintenance and deed restrictions.

NEW MASTER PLANS

Thomas Ranch & Moonlight Bend

Large new developments in planning and early build-out. Thomas Ranch is planned for about 3,500 homes. Expect construction traffic for years.

Your first-month checklist

  1. Confirm your county and school district from the property address.
  2. Find your water source: public supply, MUD, or private well.
  3. If you're on septic, get the last service record and set up pumping.
  4. Set up electric and internet. Fiber is available in some subdivisions, not all.
  5. Get a library card at Spicewood Community Library, 1011 Spur 191.
  6. File your homestead exemption with your county appraisal district.
